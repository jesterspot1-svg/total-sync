## Short description

## About

Keep your notes and files in sync between your computer and phone using your own WebDAV storage. On Windows, you can also transfer plugins and their portable settings.

Start in four steps: install TotalSync; enter your WebDAV address and login; create a cloud vault on the first device; connect your other devices using the same ID.

Choose English or Russian, sync automatically, and follow progress in the desktop status bar. Free, with no telemetry. Requires Obsidian 1.11.5+ and HTTPS WebDAV with a password or app password. Files are not encrypted by the plugin. Keep Obsidian open on iPhone while syncing.
